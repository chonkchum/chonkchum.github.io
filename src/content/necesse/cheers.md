**Cheers!** is a mod for Necesse that introduces a complete hop cultivation and brewing system. Expand your farming, brew unique beverages, and gain powerful buffs for your adventures!

### Main Features

* **Hop Cultivation:** Find hop seeds by exploring underground chests and crates in caves. Plant them, grow them, and harvest your own hops!
* **Brewing Station:** Craft a Brewery Barrel directly at the regular Workbench and use it as a special station for your brewing needs.
* **Craft Beer:** Brew 10 different types of craft beer using various ingredients such as wheat, fruits, honey, and even chili.

### Available Beers & Effects

Every beer provides unique stat bonuses, but beware – alcohol makes you slightly more vulnerable to incoming damage!
