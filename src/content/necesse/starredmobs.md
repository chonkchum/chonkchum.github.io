Breathe new life, challenge, and rewards into your Necesse world! Starred Mobs introduces powerful enemy and farm animal variants featuring 1 to 3 stars, star breeding mechanics, and boosted loot tables.

### Latest Updates

**UPDATE 1.2: The Summon & Precision Update!**
* **Summons & Mounts Fix:** Fixed an unintended bug where mounts, pets, player summons, and bees were spawning with stars. The mod now has a strict fail-safe: only mobs that actually drop loot can be starred!
* **Extreme Rarity Support:** Updated the config descriptions to clarify that you can use high-precision decimals for spawn chances. Want a super rare 1-in-10,000 boss variant? Just set the chance to `0.0001`!

**UPDATE 1.1: The Ultimate Configuration Update!**
The settings file has been completely overhauled! You now have total control over every aspect of the mod:
* **Separate Categories:** Customize spawn rates and loot multipliers independently for Normal Mobs, Bosses (disabled by default), and Farm Animals.
* **Deep Stat Customization:** Modify Health, Damage, Speed, Health Regen, Attack Speed, Crit Chance, Armor Penetration, and Flat Armor for each star tier separately.
* **Master Toggles:** Turn specific tiers, mob categories, or particle effects on/off with a single switch.

### Core Features

* **Star Tiers & Combat Buffs:** Mobs can spawn with 1, 2, or 3 stars, dynamically scaling their combat power and rewarding your victories.
* **Graphical Particles:** Starred mobs emit dynamic, fading graphical star particles matching their tier's distinct color (fully configurable).
* **Loot Multipliers & Extra Rolls:** Defeating starred mobs multiplies drop quantities, with decimal remainders rolling a fair chance for extra bonus items.
* **Breeding & Inheritance System:** Farm animals pass down their traits to offspring; children inherit the lowest star tier of both parents (e.g., 1-star + 2-star = 1-star).
* **Smart Spawning Filters:** Humans/settlers and summons are safely excluded. Bosses and normal critters can be tweaked in the settings.

### Configuration & Customization

Upon first launching the game with the mod, a configuration file is automatically created inside `%APPDATA%\Necesse\cfg\mods`.

For EACH star tier, you can independently configure:
* Max Health
* Overall Damage
* Movement Speed
* Health Regeneration
* Attack Speed
* Critical Hit Chance
* Armor Penetration
* Flat Armor
* Light Emission (Toggle)

You also have full control over Spawn Chances and Loot Multipliers for Normal Mobs, Bosses, and Farm Animals separately, as well as Particle frequencies and Master Toggles.
